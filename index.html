Welcome to Blabblepedia

Blabblepedia is the official community archive dedicated to cataloging the history, biology, and world of Blabbles across Blath.

Overview: The Blabble

A Blabble (plural: Blabbles) is a distinct species native to the world of Blath. According to ancient lore records, the first Blabbles officialy spawned in the year 503 BW (Before War), which corresponds to 1495 in standard terrestrial reckoning.

Geography: Blath

Blath (commonly referred to by inhabitants as "blabs") is the home world of the Blabbles—effectively the Blabble equivalent of Earth. It features diverse terrain, ancient spawn grounds, and the central civilization hub known as Blabbleland.

Key Historical Eras & Locations

  • 503 BW (1495): The First Spawning of the Blabbles on Blath.
  • 200 BW: The official founding and establishment of Blabbleland.

Blabbleland

Established in 200 BW, Blabbleland serves as the central region and sanctuary for all Blabbles residing on Blath. It was constructed 303 years after the initial spawning event and remains a core focus of ongoing historical record-keeping.
